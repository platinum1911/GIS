Research overview

This project compares projected monthly climatological daily maximum temperatures under SSP1-2.6 and SSP5-8.5 for the period 2081–2100, using the same climate model and spatial resolution.

2081–2100Projection period
2Emissions scenarios
12Monthly temperature layers per scenario
JapanStudy region
Interpretation note. Here “maximum temperature” refers to monthly averages of daily maximum temperature, not the single hottest temperature in a year. Scenario differences represent model projections, not observed warming.

Methods & data

1. Data preparation

Load the WorldClim CMIP6 maximum-temperature raster stacks for MRI-ESM2-0 under SSP126 and SSP585. Read Japanese city coordinates and the national boundary.

2. Spatial extraction

Crop and mask climate rasters to Japan, then extract monthly raster values at selected city point locations.

3. Descriptive statistics

Calculate city-level means across the 12 climatological months and compare scenarios with SSP5 − SSP1. Visualize spatial patterns and distributions.

Source: WorldClim CMIP6. This website is a styled presentation of the uploaded R-generated HTML; it does not rerun the model or alter numerical results.
